### Your first reps

1. Say hi on the Discord
2. Pick a [good first issue](https://github.com/search?q=org%3ASolidGeeks+label%3A%22good+first+issue%22+state%3Aopen&type=issues)
3. Sign off your commits with `git commit -s`
4. Open a pull request. A maintainer reviews it with you.
