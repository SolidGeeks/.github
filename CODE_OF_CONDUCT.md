# Code of Conduct

Solid Geeks adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies in every repository, on the Discord, and at our events.

## In our words

- **Be kind.** Critique code, never people. Everyone was a newcomer once.
- **Be rigorous.** Back opinions with facts, tests or links.
- **Assume good faith**, and say so when you are not sure.
- **No harassment**, discrimination or personal attacks. Ever.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues privately to the maintainers (contact listed on [our site](https://solidgeeks.github.io/SolidGeeks/)). Reports are handled confidentially. Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
